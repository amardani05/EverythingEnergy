UI concepts

Three directions for everything that is not the map. The Atlas plate and the dashboard-to-map handoff stay as they are. Each mock below runs on the live site data, so what you see is what the screen would actually say today. Already built in this style and live: Flows (Plate II) and Balances.

Concept A

The commodity desk

One screen per molecule. Today the site is organised by tool (map, lab, signals). A desk is organised by market: pick crude, gas, coal, uranium or power and see its balance, its trade routes, its chokepoint exposure and the equity baskets that carry it, side by side. This is the layout that makes "energy as a whole" navigable, and it gives every future balance model an obvious home.

cost: medium · needs a balance model per commodity (crude exists; gas storage is the cheapest next one) · replaces: nothing, adds a front door

Concept B

The wall

Every basket, every lens, one glance. The dashboard currently shows the top five of four separate lists, so a basket that is mildly stretched on everything never appears. A dense matrix shows all 23 baskets against all the lenses at once; colour does the ranking and anomalies show up as rows that light up across columns. Click-through goes to the existing basket drawer.

cost: low · all data already in dashboard_data.json · replaces: the four top-five lists under Active Signals

Concept C

The morning brief

Sentences, ranked by size of move. Cards make you decode a z-score, a sparkline and two tickers before you know whether to care. A brief states each finding in plain words, largest first, mixing physical (balances, flows) and financial (pairs, regime breaks) in one list. The map stays docked on the right as the way into detail, the same handoff you have now.

cost: low to medium · templated text from existing fields · replaces: the Active Signals card grid
